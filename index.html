Jorge Faggiani

Television Music Composer and Producer

I am a Television Music Composer and Producer from Uruguay. Since 2011, I have had the privilege of creating music for synchronization across international broadcasts, helping shape the sonic background for major network series and reality television formats.

Over the course of my career, my compositions have found a home in various global franchises, including America's Next Top Model, Keeping Up with the Kardashians, RuPaul's Drag Race All Stars, and The Real Housewives of Miami, as well as network projects like ABC's Blood & Oil and television documentaries.

My catalog is published and administered globally through industry partners and libraries, including Sony/ATV Tunes (Discovery Trademark Holding Company), JDE Music, and CouldB Entertainment. This has allowed my work to be regularly broadcast across networks such as The CW, Bravo, E!, VH1, MTV, and Discovery Networks.
